Legacy AI

When someone leaves a role, the knowledge that made them good at it usually leaves with them — undocumented approvals, unwritten risks, the "ask so-and-so" context no wiki ever captures. Legacy AI captures that knowledge from the outgoing employee before they're gone, has their manager validate it, and hands the successor an AI companion trained on that specific person's real context — so it can actually answer "who approves this?" instead of pointing at a stale doc.

1CaptureWhat's leaving, validated against real handover items — not guessed.
2VerifyManager signs off daily, so nothing ships to the successor unchecked.
3Hand offSuccessor asks a real AI, grounded in that context — see the "How the AI works" step for exactly what's real.
